Your head motion stays on your Mac.

This policy covers the macblurry Mac App Store app published by AMILERO DAY S.R.L.

Data processed on your device

With your permission, macblurry reads headphone motion to determine your head's direction and control a screen overlay. Motion samples and the relative head position are processed in memory on your Mac. The app does not upload or maintain a history of those samples.

Your preferences, such as gesture thresholds, display coverage, and overlay appearance, are stored locally using macOS UserDefaults. macOS manages Motion permission and launch at login settings.

Collection and sharing

The app has no account, advertising, tracking, third party analytics, or developer operated backend. It does not transmit motion, settings, or usage data to the developer or other parties. It does not capture your screen, microphone, or camera. The App Store version does not monitor or control other apps' media playback.

System diagnostics

macOS may keep local diagnostic messages about app startup, motion availability, permissions, and errors. Apple may process crash reports and App Store diagnostics according to your system settings and Apple's privacy policy. macblurry does not send its own analytics reports.

Your choices and retention

You can revoke Motion access in System Settings at any time and use manual privacy controls instead. Quitting the app stops motion processing. Preferences remain locally until the app's stored data is removed. Uninstalling an app may leave preferences on macOS.

Support requests and this website

If you open a GitHub support request, you choose what to share. Requests are public and GitHub processes them under its own policies. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record visitor technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation. We do not add analytics or advertising cookies to these pages.
