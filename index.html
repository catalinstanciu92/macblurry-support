A free utility for macOS 14 and later

Screen privacy when you look away.

macblurry lives in your menu bar. With compatible headphones, a head turn can cover your displays, then uncover them when you look back.

Need to remove privacy?

Press Command + Shift + B, press Escape while covered, click the cover, or choose Remove Privacy from the menu bar menu.

Get started

  1. Open macblurry and find its icon in the menu bar. It has no Dock icon.
  2. Connect and wear headphones that provide head motion through Apple's headphone motion API. Compatibility depends on the device; the app cannot identify a specific model.
  3. Open Settings from the menu and choose Request access. Allow Motion & Fitness access when macOS asks.
  4. Wait for Head tracking active, then use Recenter Head Position while facing your display.
  5. Use Test blur to check the effect and dismissal controls before relying on gestures.

Motion access or tracking is unavailable

Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, Motion & Fitness and allow macblurry if listed. Reconnect and wear your headphones, then choose Check again in macblurry Settings. The tracker status and Current relative yaw show whether motion samples are arriving.

You can still use Blur Now and the keyboard shortcut without headphone motion.

Choose how privacy behaves

Look away mode covers the screen after a sustained turn and removes the cover when you return. Turn and hold mode toggles privacy after a held turn. Settings let you adjust angles, hold time, direction detection, darkness, transparency, and which displays are covered.

What happens if headphones disconnect?

An active privacy cover stays in place. Use any dismissal control to remove it.

What does the cover protect?

The cover helps obscure your display from people nearby. Its appearance depends on your settings. It is not a security lock and does not guarantee protection from screenshots, screen recording, remote access, or someone dismissing it.

Does macblurry pause media?

The Mac App Store version does not pause or resume other apps' media.

Contact support

Open a support request with your macOS version, macblurry version, headphone device, and the steps that reproduce the issue. GitHub requests are public. Do not include passwords, private screenshots, or other sensitive information.
